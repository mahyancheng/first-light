# A different starting point

The core fantasy is agency in an uncertain technological industry. The player's thesis is a commitment to a problem, not a character class. The game should create a causal chain the player can understand: research → validated capability → product → served demand → operating cash → strategic freedom.

There is no old code or screen structure to preserve. The visual language is graphite, mineral white, cobalt and pale gold; a full-page workspace replaces stacked navigation drawers. The Chief of Staff is a named destination, not a floating chat bubble. The company journey derives from actual capability, customers and cash, and can return to survival under financial stress.

## Player freedom and honest limits

Research designs are composed, not a linear unlock tree. Every architecture states its supported outputs, training cost, capability and serving trade-off. Training method and dataset investment change programme economics. Uncertainty appears as evaluation setbacks; known resource constraints remain visible. Advanced actions are not locked behind arbitrary experience levels.

Negotiation separates intent, counteroffer, review and commitment. A player's cap is retained in the request, even when a seller counters above it. Schedules and cancellation terms are explicit. Multiple accepted orders compete for real supplier capacity. Binding terms are produced by the simulation, not invented by conversation.

The first playable deliberately concentrates on running an AI company. It does not pretend a shallow placeholder is an IPO, political system or acquisition engine. The README lists these unimplemented systems explicitly. Future systems must enter through the same validation, accounting, authority and receipt model.

## Required acceptance cases

- Research to launch to revenue across multiple deterministic quarter traces.
- Cash conservation every quarter, including borrowing, principal and dilution.
- No overselling supplier capacity; exact delivery schedules and cancellation rules.
- Identical request replay commits once; stale revisions and conflicting identities reject.
- Model failure, restart and delayed reply preserve messages without fabricating delivery.
- Readable phone navigation, visible advisor and complete quote terms.
- Build and deployment from a GitHub commit, with no Mac runtime dependency.

## Economic interpretation

Customer counts represent business accounts with quarterly subscriptions. Demand is split against competitor offers and an outside option by quality, relative price and awareness. Retention constrains immediate growth, while engineering support and allocated inference compute constrain served demand. Payroll and reserved compute are recurring obligations, while research setup, recruiting and launch costs consume cash when committed. Capital is not revenue. Scenario calibration is fictional and requires continued balancing through playtests; passing accounting tests alone does not establish realism.
